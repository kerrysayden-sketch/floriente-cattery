## Questions and Answers

**How can I verify these testimonials are real?**

You can't — by reading this page. That's the honest answer. What you can do is ask us directly to put you in touch with one of these families. Several have agreed to talk with people who are still deciding. A real conversation with a real family says more than any screenshot.

On our Instagram [@floriente_orientals_cattery](https://instagram.com/floriente_orientals_cattery) we regularly post photos of our graduates — you can see these cats in their new homes. The families don't run separate accounts, but on our feed you can spot many of them and watch how their cats grow a year, two, three years after the move.

**Can I get in touch with other families?**

Yes. Write to us — we'll ask on your behalf. Not every family is available, but some are. It's the most direct verification we can offer: not a screenshot, but a conversation.

**What pushed these owners toward Floriente?**

Different things. Tetiana came after a loss — she needed documents and a healthy kitten. Stas came after research — he needed transparency and access. Nataliia came after a cold experience — she wanted to feel the difference. Yulia found us on OLX. The starting points differ. The pattern doesn't.

**Why do families come back for a second cat?**

Nobody comes back to a cattery they don't trust. Nobody buys a cat from someone who got it wrong the first time. Repeat buyers are a signal that doesn't lie.

**What does "Ориенталов мало не бывает" mean?**

It's Elvira's phrase. Loosely translated: "You can never have too many Orientals." She repeats it because it keeps proving true. One family has three. Another is planning a second. The breed creates its own demand. Once you take one — you understand why.
