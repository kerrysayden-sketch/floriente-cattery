At home she has any number of affectionate names, and she answers to all of them. Yellow and black paws, and a nose to match - as though someone had painted her with two brushes at once.

She is never demanding: she always seems to be checking, "Did I do that right? Is this allowed?" Pick her up and she purrs and cuddles close. In the evening she has her own routine: settling comfortably into someone's arms for a while, then heading off to sleep with Halva - the two of them curled up together.

Gentle, affectionate, tactful.
